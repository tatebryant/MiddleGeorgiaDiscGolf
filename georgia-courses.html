Favorite Georgia Disc Golf Courses

Five personally recommended, premier road trip destinations across Georgia that showcase the best of Southern disc golf architecture and natural terrain.

Personally Tested & Recommended

The Best Road Trips in Georgia

While Middle Georgia offers incredible local tracks in Macon and Warner Robins, Georgia is packed with championship-level courses worth loading up the car for a Saturday morning day trip. The five courses below represent my personal favorite destinations in the state. Each offers a unique blend of world-class design, dramatic elevation, water hazards, and memorable shot-shaping challenges.

The Top 5 Personal Favorites

Detailed specifications, architectural highlights, verified hole counts, and honest reviews from the tee.

#1 Ranked in GA
Carrollton, GA • Carroll County

1. Hobbs Farm Disc Golf Course

Holes: 20 Holes Par: 68–70 Designer: John Houck Tees: Concrete with Stacked Stone Targets: Innova DISCcatcher Pro Cost: Free Public City Park

Designed by renowned course architect John Houck, Hobbs Farm is consistently rated as the premier disc golf experience in Georgia and one of the finest public courses in the United States. Situated in Carrollton, the layout features magnificent hand-laid stacked stone tee pads, manicured meadow fairways, babbling creek crossings, and an unforgettable island green.

Personal Review & Recommendation

"Hobbs Farm is hands-down the best disc golf experience in the state. From the moment you step onto the elevated stone tee pad on hole 1, you realize this is a true championship venue. You do not need to throw 450 feet to score well here, but you absolutely must throw your fairway drivers on your intended angles. A must-play road trip from Macon."

Epic Elevation
Dacula, GA • Gwinnett County

2. Little Mulberry Park

Holes: 18 Holes Par: 57–60 Tees: Dual Concrete (Red / Blue) Targets: DISCcatcher Pro Terrain: Heavy Elevation / Mountain-Style Cost: Free County Park

Located within an 890-acre park in Gwinnett County, Little Mulberry Park delivers dramatic Piedmont terrain rarely found in central Georgia. The course is celebrated for its severe elevation changes, requiring players to compensate for massive uphill grinds and exhilarating downhill bombs. Beautiful mature hardwoods define the fairway corridors, rewarding precise placement over pure arm strength.

Personal Review & Recommendation

"Hole 18 at Little Mulberry is one of the most exciting finishing holes in disc golf: a steep downhill throw that feels like dropping off a mountain cliff into the hardwood valley below. Bring sturdy hiking shoes and leave your high-speed understable discs in the car—the downhill wind will turn them over into the rough."

27 Championship Holes
Stockbridge, GA • Henry County

3. J.P. Moseley Park DGC

Holes: 27 Holes Par: 84–90 Tees: Concrete (Multiple Layouts) Targets: Innova DISCcatcher Terrain: Rolling Hills & Lake Hazard Cost: Free County Park

Located in Stockbridge just off I-75 south of Atlanta, J.P. Moseley Park is an iconic championship complex that has hosted top regional and national tournaments. Featuring 27 full holes spread over expansive rolling hills, manicured fairways, and challenging water hazards, Moseley tests every throw in a player's arsenal. It provides a true test of stamina and mental focus.

Personal Review & Recommendation

"Because it is directly off I-75 between Macon and Atlanta, J.P. Moseley is our favorite halfway stop. It is a true bomber track on the open holes, but the wooded back loops and water hazards keep you honest. Playing all 27 holes is an absolute workout, but completely worth every throw."

Hidden Gem
Louisville, GA • Jefferson County

4. Ogeechee Crossing DiscGolfPark

Holes: 18 Holes Par: 63 Design: DiscGolfPark Tees: Artificial Turf / Concrete Targets: DiscGolfPark Pro Targets Cost: Free Public Access

Nestled along the banks of the historic Ogeechee River in rural Jefferson County, Ogeechee Crossing is widely hailed by Georgia players as the state's best hidden gem. Designed to professional DiscGolfPark standards, it features demanding multi-shot par 4s and par 5s requiring disciplined landing zones, natural cypress swamp scenery, and scenic river views.

Personal Review & Recommendation

"Ogeechee Crossing is raw, secluded Georgia nature at its finest. You will feel completely separated from civilization. The layout challenges you to manage risk and reward along the riverbanks. Be sure to bag plenty of bug spray during summer rounds!"

Pure Woods Golf
Between, GA • Walton County

5. Heritage Pines

Holes: 18 Holes Par: 56–58 Tees: Dual Concrete (Blue / Red) Targets: DISCcatcher Pro Style: Technical Pine Plantation Cost: Free Public Park

Located in the charming community of Between, Georgia, Heritage Pines is the quintessential Georgia woods course. Playing through dense planted pine forests and old-growth hardwood canopies, the fairways form pristine needle-covered corridors where hitting your designated gap is paramount. Dual tee pads accommodate both advancing beginners and seasoned scratch players.

Personal Review & Recommendation

"If you love pure woods disc golf where distance takes a backseat to gap-hitting accuracy, Heritage Pines is your paradise. The pine needle footing is pristine, and throwing a flat laser shot down a 300-foot pine tunnel here is one of the most satisfying feelings in disc golf."
